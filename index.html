How to Price Your Freelance Projects Correctly

Many freelancers make the mistake of charging based on simple hourly targets without taking non-billable overhead into account. Admin work, client communication, self-employment taxes, and software costs often consume 30% to 40% of working hours.

Using a target net-income model guarantees that business operational costs are covered prior to profit taking, ensuring long-term freelance sustainability.
